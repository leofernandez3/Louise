About Louise

A patient guide to the French language

Bonjour! I'm Louise — a Paris-born teacher with over 12 years of experience helping students from around the world fall in love with French. I hold a Master's in French Literature and the DAEFLE certification for teaching French as a Foreign Language.

My approach is warm, structured and entirely personalised. Whether you're preparing for the DELF, relocating to France, or simply want to chat with confidence, I'll meet you where you are.

  • Master's in French Literature · DAEFLE certified (FLE)
  • Specialist in DELF / DALF exam preparation
  • Business & professional French for companies
  • Lessons in English support or full French immersion
